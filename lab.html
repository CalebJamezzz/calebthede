Half-formed, in motion.

Experiments you can actually touch — interactive toys I've built, and the clever tests I find out in the wild and want to keep.

Orbit is the looser half of the Atlas: things that aren't finished products but are worth charting anyway. Some are built here — small interactive pieces you can launch right on the page. Others are found — experiments and demos from elsewhere that earned a place on the map. Topics overlap freely: a thing can be psychology and a game at once.
